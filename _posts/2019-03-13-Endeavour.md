Dr Gan is among the recipients of the recently announced [Endeavour](http://internationaleducation.gov.au/endeavour) Executive Leadership Awards, supported by Australian Government Department of Education and Training. This award will enable him to work with Professor Dominic Vella at the Mathematical Institute in the University of Oxford, on project related to spontaneous directional transport (SDT) of liquids. This collaboration will aim to develop engineering solutions towards harvesting water from atmospheric humidity for addressing freshwater scarcity across the globe.
