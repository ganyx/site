The Australian Academy of Science has announced the successful recipients of the Australia–India Strategic Research Fund (AISRF) Early- and Mid-Career Researcher (EMCR) 2019 [Fellowships](https://www.science.org.au/news-and-events/news-and-media-releases/australian-researchers-collaborate-india-science-projects). Dr Yixiang Gan was named among 11 recipients from different universities in Australia. He will be working with colleagues from IIT Madras and IIT Bhubaneswar on “Optimising thermal energy storage with phase change materials: with applications to solar energy storage”.
